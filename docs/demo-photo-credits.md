# Demo photo credits

under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). They are hot-linked from
`images.unsplash.com`, as Unsplash asks, and listed in `prisma/demo-images.ts`. A real store
replaces them by uploading its own photos in the admin panel; `pnpm setup:store` creates no demo
products.
